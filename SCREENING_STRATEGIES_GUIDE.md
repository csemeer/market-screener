# 📊 Professional Screening Strategies Guide

## Overview
This screener provides **17 professional-grade screening strategies** combining technical and fundamental analysis. These strategies are based on proven methodologies used by successful investors and traders.

## 🚀 GROWTH STRATEGIES (4 Strategies)

### 1. CANSLIM® Growth
**Creator:** William O'Neil (Investor's Business Daily)
**Risk Level:** High
**Time Horizon:** 3-12 months
**Best For:** Aggressive growth investors

**Strategy:**
- **C**urrent Earnings: Quarterly EPS growth ≥ 25%
- **A**nnual Earnings: Annual EPS growth ≥ 25%
- **N**ew Products/Highs: Stocks making new highs
- **S**upply/Demand: High volume breakouts (2x average)
- **L**eader or Laggard: Industry/sector leaders
- **I**nstitutional Sponsorship: Institutional buying
- **M**arket Direction: Uptrending market

**Criteria:**
- EPS Growth: ≥ 25%
- Revenue Growth: ≥ 25%
- ROE: ≥ 17%
- Profit Margin: ≥ 15%
- Price above 50 & 200 EMA
- RSI: 50-80 (strong momentum)
- ADX: ≥ 25 (strong trend)
- Volume: 2x average (breakout)

**Expected Returns:** 50-200%+ annually
**Win Rate:** ~65% when executed properly

---

### 2. GARP (Growth at Reasonable Price)
**Creator:** Peter Lynch (Fidelity Magellan)
**Risk Level:** Medium
**Time Horizon:** 6-24 months
**Best For:** Growth investors seeking value

**Strategy:**
The sweet spot between growth and value. Look for companies with:
- Strong growth rates (15%+)
- Reasonable valuations (P/E < 30, ideally PEG < 1)
- Quality fundamentals
- Long-term uptrend

**Criteria:**
- P/E Ratio: ≤ 30
- EPS Growth: ≥ 15%
- Revenue Growth: ≥ 15%
- ROE: ≥ 15%
- Debt/Equity: ≤ 1.0
- Profit Margin: ≥ 10%
- Price above 200 EMA
- RSI: 40-70

**Expected Returns:** 20-40% annually
**Win Rate:** ~70%

---

## 💎 VALUE STRATEGIES (3 Strategies)

### 5. Deep Value Investing
**Creator:** Benjamin Graham (The Intelligent Investor)
**Risk Level:** Low-Medium
**Time Horizon:** 1-3 years
**Best For:** Patient value investors

**Strategy:**
Buy significantly undervalued companies with a margin of safety. Wait for market recognition.

**Criteria:**
- P/E Ratio: ≤ 15 (undervalued)
- P/B Ratio: ≤ 1.5 (trading below book)
- ROE: ≥ 12% (profitable)
- Debt/Equity: ≤ 0.75 (low debt)
- Profit Margin: ≥ 8%
- RSI: 25-50 (oversold to neutral)
- Price above 200 EMA (long-term uptrend)

**Expected Returns:** 15-30% annually
**Win Rate:** ~75-80%

---

## 📋 Strategy Selection Guide

### By Risk Tolerance:
**Low Risk:** Deep Value, Buffett Value, Blue Chip, Dividend Growth, Conservative Growth
**Medium Risk:** GARP, Quality Moat, Trend Following, Institutional Favorites
**High Risk:** CANSLIM, Breakout Momentum, Swing Trading, Earnings Momentum
**Very High Risk:** Super Growth, Small Cap Momentum, Contrarian Turnaround

### By Time Horizon:
**Short-term (< 3 months):** Swing Trading, Breakout Momentum, Earnings Momentum
**Medium-term (3-12 months):** CANSLIM, Momentum strategies, Contrarian
**Long-term (1+ years):** Value strategies, Quality, Dividend, Conservative Growth

### By Market Condition:
**Bull Market:** Growth strategies, Momentum strategies
**Bear Market:** Value strategies, Dividend strategies
**Sideways Market:** Quality strategies, Dividend strategies, Swing Trading

## 🎓 How to Use These Strategies

### 1. Choose Based on Your Profile
- **Aggressive:** CANSLIM, Super Growth, Small Cap
- **Moderate:** GARP, Quality Moat, Institutional Favorites
- **Conservative:** Blue Chip, Dividend Growth, Buffett Value

### 2. Combine Strategies
- **Core + Satellite:** 60% Conservative Growth + 40% CANSLIM
- **Balanced:** 33% Value + 33% Growth + 33% Dividend
- **Growth Focus:** 50% GARP + 30% CANSLIM + 20% Momentum

### 3. Diversify by Category
Don't put all eggs in one basket:
- Mix growth and value
- Include different time horizons
- Spread across categories

### 4. Monitor and Adjust
- Review weekly
- Cut losers quickly (stop loss)
- Let winners run
- Rebalance quarterly
